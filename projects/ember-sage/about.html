Our Story

Fire, Ingredient,
and Hospitality.

Ember & Sage was created around one relationship: fire, fresh ingredients, modern technique, and genuine hospitality.

How We Began

A Restaurant Built Around a Single Idea

Ember & Sage opened in Lakeside, Pokhara in 2019 with a simple conviction: that the best cooking happens close to the fire and close to the ingredient. We built our kitchen around a live-flame hearth and our menu around what the season actually gives us — not the other way around.

The name reflects that balance. Ember is heat, transformation, intensity. Sage is calm, herbal, grounding. Every dish we serve tries to hold both at once.

Meet the Chef

Chef Aarav Sharma

Executive Chef

Aarav trained in kitchens across Kathmandu and Southeast Asia before returning to Pokhara to build a menu rooted in the valley's own produce. His cooking favors restraint over spectacle — modern technique used quietly, in service of the ingredient rather than the plate's presentation.

He works directly with a small group of growers and fishers, changes the menu with the season, and still expedites the pass most nights of the week.
